## Core model
- Each package: 6 sessions, 45–60 minutes each.
- Initial diagnostic assessment: 150–250 SAR.
- Session fees: 250–350 SAR depending on specialization.
- Hydrotherapy excluded.
- Package framework states alignment with expert PT standards and RCJY clinical quality.

## Standard six-session flow
1. Assessment: diagnosis, baseline testing, pain/function scale
2. Initial intervention: exercises + patient education
3–4. Strength & mobility: progressive resistance + core stabilization
5. Functional training: movement simulation + real-life tasks
6. Evaluation/discharge: outcome test + home plan + physician feedback
