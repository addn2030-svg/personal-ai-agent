How I work

My clinical approach emphasizes evidence from movement and testing rather than assumptions. A useful intervention should produce a measurable change that can be reassessed.

Observe
Start with the dysfunctional movement or meaningful task.
Test
Use focused clinical tests to narrow the likely driver.
Reason
Separate findings, hypotheses, and uncertainty.
Intervene
Choose the smallest relevant intervention.
Reassess
Confirm whether the movement or symptom changed.

MyoMentor AI

Clinical decision-support project

  • Structured therapist registration, new-case, and follow-up workflows.
  • Pre-visit intelligent intake to reduce wasted assessment time.
  • Clinical reasoning support while keeping the clinician responsible for decisions.
  • Outcome tracking and reassessment logic rather than one-time documentation.
Clinical Method

Fascial Functional Assessment

Practice framework

A functional assessment approach that considers movement quality, muscle performance, load transfer, tissue response, and relevant fascial relationships without treating fascia as an isolated explanation for every symptom.

  • Begins with the patient’s meaningful movement dysfunction.
  • Uses focused testing to identify relevant contributors.
  • Integrates fascial findings only when supported by movement evidence.
  • Requires reassessment after intervention to confirm relevance.
