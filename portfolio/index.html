How I work

My clinical reasoning emphasizes evidence from movement and test–retest response. I avoid assuming that the location of pain is automatically the causal source.

Observe
Start with the dysfunctional movement or meaningful functional task.
Test
Use focused clinical tests to narrow the likely contributor or driver.
Hypothesize
Separate findings, assumptions, and uncertainty.
Confirm
Use confirmation or therapy localization where clinically applicable.
Intervene
Choose the smallest relevant intervention.
Retest
Repeat the same meaningful test and document the response.

Healthcare AI

MyoMentor AI

Clinical decision-support project

MyoMentor AI extends the MyoMentor / Integrated Driver System concept into structured digital clinical support. The aim is to organize therapist testing, pre-visit information, case assessment, follow-up, and reassessment while preserving clinician responsibility for final decisions.

  • Structured new-case and follow-up workflows.
  • Pre-visit intelligent intake to prepare the therapist before the appointment.
  • Movement-first reasoning and test–retest logic.
  • Clinical decision support rather than autonomous diagnosis.
  • Outcome tracking and structured clinical knowledge capture.
Clinical Method

Fascial Functional Assessment

Integrated assessment work

A functional assessment approach examining movement quality, muscle performance, load transfer, tissue response, and relevant fascial relationships within the broader clinical picture.

  • Starts with the patient’s meaningful movement dysfunction.
  • Uses focused testing to identify clinically relevant contributors.
  • Integrates fascial findings only when supported by functional evidence.
  • Requires reassessment after intervention to test whether the finding mattered.
